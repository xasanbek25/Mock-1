SAT Math Practice Test

Module 1 and Module 2, 22 questions each, 35 minutes per module.

Before you begin

  • Enter your name once. It is used on your results.
  • Module 1 starts when you click Start. When it ends, Module 2 is unlocked and you cannot return to Module 1.
  • Use Mark for review to flag questions. The navigator lets you move between questions.
  • Desmos and the Reference sheet are available in both modules.
  • After Module 2, you will see your estimated score (200 to 800) and a review of your mistakes in both modules.

Module 1 is complete

Module 1 is now closed. Module 2 is ready to begin. The 35-minute timer starts when you click Begin Module 2.
